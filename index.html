Automation Pipeline

5 tasks, zero manual work

Every step in the recruitment journey runs automatically the moment a candidate submits their application.

01
CV Screening
GPT-4 evaluates each CV across 8 criteria and scores the candidate 0–100. A Notion candidate card is created instantly.
GPT-4 · Notion
02
Auto Interview Booking
Candidates scoring 70+ get a Google Calendar invite with Zoom link sent automatically — no HR involvement needed.
Google Calendar · Gmail
03
Smart Rejection
Scores below 50 receive a personalized, respectful rejection email referencing specific gaps identified in their CV.
OpenAI · Gmail
04
Hire / No-Hire AI Brief
After the interview, the system generates a structured hire/no-hire recommendation from interviewer notes using AI analysis.
OpenAI · Telegram
05
30-Day Onboarding Automation
Hired candidates get a full onboarding checklist created in Notion, with daily task reminders sent via Telegram throughout their first month. All 30 days of onboarding managed without a single manual message.
Notion · Telegram · Gmail
